Access to your library

FableGear needs permission to read your Rekordbox database and write backups. Your data never leaves your machine — everything runs locally.

⚠️
Full Disk Access may be required macOS restricts reading files in protected folders like ~/Library. If FableGear can't read your database, grant Full Disk Access to Terminal (or FableGear) in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Full Disk Access.
